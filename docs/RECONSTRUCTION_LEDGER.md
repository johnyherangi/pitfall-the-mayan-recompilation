The original itself, run under Wine 9 (no CD: `Option_Slow`) and captured at 20 frames a second for five minutes,
matches the port frame by frame through the whole attract run: the title with its pan and blinking prompt, the
three demos (Ceiba Jungle, Xibalba Falls, the 2600 level), the titles between them and the intro, every pixel of
every frame but the boot, the dark red the original shows while a level's files load (0.5 s of disk time the
port does not take) and the demos' blinking "DEMONSTRATION" (the timer's count, `DAT_00450fa8`, wall time).
So do its runs of levels 0 to 13, 16 to 18 and 20 (the 2600 level) picked from the menu with the right
arrow held from 14 s, 50 s each with their deaths, restarts and continue screens: the port started from the menu at
the tick the original's click came (its intro's ticks, which the logic's tick count and the clouds go by) and the key
at the original's tick shows every pixel of the original's frames but for the boot and the loads.  Where pictures
say too little, the original's memory read while it runs (Harry's and the logic's `0x465800 .. 0x4668ff`, the object
records, the random generator, the joypad) is compared with the port's tick by tick, the port given the original's
joypad tick by tick (`re/verify`): all 18 levels of the menu (0 to 13, 16 to 18 and 20), 50 s each with the right
arrow held, the whip, jumps and sling shots (the vaults with their levers), their kills, deaths, restarts and continue
screens, match every byte of the logic's memory and of the 80 records (the free ones too, their pointers included) on
every tick whose end the sampler saw (97 to 100% of them).

## The Genesis-derived game logic as a whole, and the builds

The machine-translated 68000 half of the binary (`0x401000–0x437fff`) is ported routine by routine: Harry's
states, the object system and 140 of its behaviour types, the camera and spawner, the triggers and level
scripts, the map screen, the 2600 level, the continue screen, the title, the cutscenes, level 24, the engine's
transitions, CD audio, the typed cheat codes, the parallax's water effects and the original's window (menu, status
bar, dialogs, property sheet) and the disc through MCI.  The Windows build is checked with MSVC 14.50 (Visual Studio
2026, the 10.0.26100 SDK) at `/W4 /permissive-` with no warnings, its `ph_tests` giving the g++ build's results and
its game running (cl, lib, rc and link run under Wine 6 with MSVC's own `vcruntime140_1.dll`, whose C++ exception
handling Wine 6's lacks), and with clang / llvm-mingw.

The Genesis half's own palette fades (`DAT_0046655e` .. `DAT_00466568`: the death colours, the continue screen's
match, a boss's) are written but never read on the PC (`FUN_004263e3`, the Genesis routine that ran them, is a
`ret`), so they have no effect, nor do the 1-up's stray writes into their tables (`0x41ff34` / `0x41ff56` write
the word 1 at `a0 + 0x5a` / `+ 0x6a`, a0 left in the sparkle table by the same tick's sparkle).  `FUN_00434815`,
the Genesis seeding of the random generator, is unreachable too: its state starts at zero and runs on across
games (the demos advance it too).

## Known gaps in the asset layer

* Not every `.trg` id and class byte has a name: the code tests them as the original does, and `kTriggerClass`
  is the original's table (`0x461f8e`) as it is.
* `.als` object types no level places (58, 77, 94) have no handler of their own.
